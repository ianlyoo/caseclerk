# Fresh-reader test (no prior context)
You are a first-time visitor. You know nothing about this product. You'll see screenshots of one web product: a landing page, an inbox, a case page waiting for approval, a case page after approval, and a resolved case page.

Pass 1, at 640 px wide (files `*-640.png`, in the order given): give each image a 5-second glance, then write exactly 3 sentences.
(1) What does this product do, and for whom?
(2) What on screen proves it works for real?
(3) What is unproven or unclear?

Pass 2, at 1280 px wide (files `*-1280.png`): answer briefly.
(a) Who decides when money moves: the software or the user? Where does it say so?
(b) What is the headline result, and compared to what?
(c) Which numbers are real measurements and which are samples or targets?
(d) What would you click first, and why?
(e) One thing that confused you.

Write your answers to `answers.md` in your folder. Be blunt. Don't guess at things you can't see.
